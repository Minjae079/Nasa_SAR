HOW IT WORKS

From globe to ground.

Use your mouse or touch to rotate the 3D Earth. Click any location to fly toward it and open a zoomable local map. The Illinois Tech shortcut takes you straight to the planned study area.

Use ASF search results to display two matched GCOV observation previews. Numeric change detection requires further processing.

This is a learning/prototyping interface. It does not yet detect surface changes. Verify hackathon pre-work rules before incorporating code into a competition submission.
